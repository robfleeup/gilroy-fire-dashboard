About Gilroy Fire Department

Gilroy Fire Department provides all-hazards emergency response, emergency medical services, fire suppression, rescue, community risk reduction, and regional mutual aid from three permanent fire stations and one interim response facility.

Santa Teresa Interim Fire Station

Southwest Gilroy interim response location
Flexible Assignment
Engine 50Engine 650Rescue Medic 49

The interim station provides fire and emergency medical response to southwest Gilroy while the permanent Santa Teresa Fire Station is under construction. Depending on daily staffing availability and operational needs, the station may be staffed with Engine 50, Engine 650, or Rescue Medic 49, providing flexible deployment to meet community demand and maintain citywide coverage.
